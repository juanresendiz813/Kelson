Q: A train leaves a station at 3:15pm traveling 60 mph. A second train leaves the same station at 4:00pm on the same track traveling 80 mph. At what time does the second train catch the first?
A: Let t be the time in hours after 3:15pm when the second train catches up.

Distance = speed × time.

Distance covered by the first train = 60 × (t + 0.25) because it had a 25 minute head start, which is 25/60 = 0.25 hours.

Distance covered by the second train = 80 × t.

They meet when those distances are equal:

60 × (t + 0.25) = 80 × t
60t + 15 = 80t
15 = 20t
t = 0.75 hours = 45 minutes.

So the second train catches up 45 minutes after it left, at 4:00pm + 45 minutes = 4:45pm.
